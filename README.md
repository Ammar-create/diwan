# Diwan (دیوان)

> An intimate, tactile classical Urdu poetry companion, calligraphy digitizer, and personal manuscript folio for Android.

Built with **Jetpack Compose**, **Material 3**, and authentic Nastaliq typography. Designed strictly under the **Apple Fluid Design** and **Unslop** craft standards.

---

## Highlights

* **Personal Manuscript Folio:** A quiet, uncluttered personal sanctuary for 3–4 verses that hit deep, rendered on warm parchment (`#FDFBF7`) with **Noto Nastaliq Urdu** cascading at a generous 2.2 line-height.
* **On-Device Romanization Layer:** Instant toggle in the header unrolls rhythmic phonetic Roman Urdu underneath each misra so you can recite verses smoothly.
* **Calligraphy Vision OCR:** Snap a photo or paste an image from your clipboard; the vision model parses complex handwritten or printed ligatures, extracts the verses into clean Nastaliq, and generates Roman Urdu and English translation.
* **Tughra Story Exporter (طغریٰ):** One-tap publication canvas with live aspect ratio switching (9:16 Instagram Story and 1:1 Square) with illuminated gold foil rims.
* **Poet’s Salon (محفلِ سخن):** Deep conversational companion to debate bahr (meter), explore taqtee', and discuss classical Persian and Urdu metaphors.
* **Android 13+ Themed Adaptive Icon:** Fully responsive to Android's Dynamic Color / Monet wallpaper theming with a dedicated monochrome vector layer.

## Typography & Design Tokens

* **Urdu Script:** Noto Nastaliq Urdu (`NotoNastaliqUrdu.ttf`)
* **Latin Display:** Instrument Serif (`InstrumentSerif-Regular.ttf` & `InstrumentSerif-Italic.ttf`)
* **Body & UI:** Inter (`Inter-Regular.ttf` & `Inter-Bold.ttf`)
* **Palette:**
  * Parchment: `#FDFBF7`
  * Lapis Lazuli Navy: `#0D3B66`
  * Metallic Gold Foil: `#C5A059`
  * Ink Slate: `#1E242B`
  * Garnet Ruby: `#8A1C28`
